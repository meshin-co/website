# GEO baseline — structural readiness of the 30 buyer prompts

Generated-from: 3e436cd (the commit that recorded this baseline, before its generator stamped a revision)

**This is structural readiness, not a citation baseline. No AI engine was queried.** The
protocol in `docs/SEMANTIC-CORE.md` asks for each prompt to be run through ChatGPT, Claude,
Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode and recorded as mentioned, cited or absent. That
measurement is external and manual, and it belongs in its own artifact. Phase 6 must not diff
engine behaviour against the readiness score below.

Generated by `npm run audit:geo` from the shipped build. This measures only what the site
controls: does the must-win route exist, does it ship the passage the semantic core promises,
does it carry a marked citable unit (`data-answer` or `data-gate-lead`), a JSON-LD graph and an
`llms.txt` line. A missing marked unit does not mean the page has no answer: it means extraction
has to guess which paragraph is the answer.

Structural readiness: **26 of 30** prompts have everything the site can give.
