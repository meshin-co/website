question: "What if the AI we're building goes into a product we sell, not just an internal workflow?"
answer: 'Both are covered, and the obligations differ. Building AI into a product you sell can make your company a provider rather than a deployer under the EU AI Act. That carries a higher compliance bar than using AI only inside the business. I record that distinction in the risk register from the first review, so it gets decided early, not discovered after the feature has shipped.'
