question: 'What do NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act each require of a company our size?'
answer: "None of the three sets a headcount threshold. NIST's AI RMF is voluntary guidance built around four functions: govern, map, measure and manage. ISO/IEC 42001 is a certifiable standard for running an AI management system. The EU AI Act is binding law: what you owe depends on whether you are a provider or a deployer, and the system's risk tier, not your size."
