shortDefinition: ISO/IEC 42001 is an international standard that sets out what an AI management system must do. That means the policies an organisation follows to govern its own AI, checked on a cycle rather than written once. It was published in December 2023.
definition: |
  ISO/IEC's joint technical committee for artificial intelligence published it in December 2023. It specifies requirements for an AI management system: the recurring policies, roles and processes that govern how an organisation develops, provides or uses AI. It uses the same Plan-Do-Check-Act structure as other ISO management-system standards, such as ISO/IEC 27001 for information security.

  Unlike the NIST AI RMF, a free framework, ISO/IEC 42001 is a standard a company can be audited and certified against by an accredited third party. Enterprise customers and procurement teams ask for it, or ask where you sit against it, because certification gives them a documented, externally checked answer instead of a vendor's own claim.

  In practice, working towards it means three things: writing down a risk-assessment method, running an inventory of AI systems, and reviewing that inventory on a schedule. None of that requires certification itself.

## Why it comes up

A procurement team building a vendor shortlist asks for it by name. A board that already understands ISO 27001 reaches for the same mental model, and asks whether an AI equivalent exists. Because it is a certifiable standard, not a public framework, it carries more weight in a formal audit than a self-written policy. That is why a customer's legal team may put it on a checklist before reading what is in it.

## What to do about it

Full certification is a real project with an external auditor, and most companies at seed to growth stage do not need it yet. What is worth doing now is running the standard's own structure without the audit: a documented risk-assessment method, an AI system inventory, and a review of that inventory on a fixed schedule. That gives you "built to the same structure, without the certificate," a stronger position than silence or an unsupported claim.
